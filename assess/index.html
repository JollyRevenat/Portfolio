Who it's for

For companies that need to know their people know it.

Training & onboarding

Your teams build the checks, your people take them, and you see who's ready and who needs help.

  • Onboarding checks. Make sure new starters have taken in the essentials, from safety to systems.
  • Compliance & training assessments. Scored assessments with a clear record of who took what, and how they did.
  • Team surveys & polls. Ask how people feel, what's working and what isn't, and get an honest read.
Also useful for

Engaging your audience

Put outcome quizzes and polls on your website, embed them anywhere, and capture leads from the people who take them.
